Our atelier

Mechanical honesty, custom stance, and a finish that feels intentional.

Bandit Garage is positioned as an atelier first. The brand language is short, direct and confident, so this redesign keeps that energy while making the story easier to read: custom builds, signature parts, apparel and a workshop culture that feels international without losing its Portuguese base.

The result is a homepage with more gravity, more rhythm and more visual depth. It gives the motorcycles the space they deserve and turns the brand into something that feels premium from the first scroll.

Process

From brief to finished machine.

The clearest line on the live site comes from the atelier page: it all starts with your idea. This redesign turns that into a full process flow so visitors immediately understand how the workshop works.
